## Embedded Google Fonts

Inter Regular 400 and Medium 500 and JetBrains Mono Regular 400 were downloaded from the official Google Fonts stylesheet and fonts.gstatic.com on 30 September 2026, converted to WOFF2 without glyph subsetting, and embedded in the shared CSS. They require no network request in the source or bundled prototype. License texts: `Inter-OFL.txt` and `JetBrains-Mono-OFL.txt`, from the corresponding Google Fonts repository directories. Archivo Narrow Semibold and the Material Symbols subset remain embedded; Neue Montreal retains the existing embedded files.

## Updated dotted-body illustrations

`body-female.webp` and `body-male.webp` are quality-94 WebP derivatives of the user-supplied `Female Bust 2.png` and `Male-bust 1.png`. Full source dimensions and originals are retained. Shared contained framing, multiply blending and a lower-edge alpha mask blend their white backgrounds into each surface. The published artifact embeds both derivatives.
